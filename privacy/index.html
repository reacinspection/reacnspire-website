Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information.

English · Español
Effective October 26, 2026

Kendra REAC Consultant, Inc. ("Kendra REAC," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy applies to reacnspire.com and information submitted through our consultation, training, registration, contact, and email subscription forms. Separate products or applications may provide additional privacy notices.

Information we collect

  • Contact information such as your name, email address, phone number, company, and preferred contact method.
  • Property and service details, including location, unit count, inspection dates, prior scores, and information you include in messages.
  • Transaction information processed by Square. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number.
  • Basic website usage information, device details, referral source, and interactions collected through Metricool and standard hosting logs.
  • Information you voluntarily provide by email, phone, forms, or during a business engagement.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, schedule and deliver services, process registrations, provide support, improve our website and offerings, maintain business records, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable obligations.

Service providers and sharing

We may share information only as needed with providers that support our operations, including Netlify for website hosting and forms, Google Workspace for business communications, Square for payments, Metricool for website analytics, and Brevo for email subscriptions and communications. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transaction. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and local storage

The site may use browser storage to remember language or promotional-message preferences. Analytics providers may use cookies or similar technologies. You can restrict these through your browser, although some features may not work as intended.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, contractual and accounting records, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, or ask us to stop non-essential marketing messages. We may retain records when required for legitimate business, contractual, or legal purposes.

Children and external links

Our services are intended for business professionals and are not directed to children under 13. Our website links to third-party services whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Email subscriptions and Brevo

This Privacy Policy also applies to our NSPIREPro email subscription forms and related subscription communications.

Information we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your email address, the market where you manage properties (Florida, Puerto Rico, or both), your preferred email language, and your consent. You may also provide your name, organization, and the apps that interest you. We maintain subscription records, including available sign-up and confirmation dates, the form and consent wording used, and unsubscribe requests.

How subscriptions work

We use this information to manage your subscription and send tips about inspections and maintenance, app demonstrations, product updates, and offers from Kendra REAC Consultant, Inc. We send up to two marketing emails per month. A subscription-confirmation email is separate from that limit.

We use double opt-in: after submitting a subscription form, you receive an email asking you to confirm. You join the selected marketing list only after clicking the confirmation link. If you did not request the subscription, you can ignore that email. No app purchase is required to subscribe. A publicly listed business email address alone does not constitute permission to join this subscription list.

Our email service provider

We use Brevo to host subscription forms, manage contact lists and preferences, send subscription and marketing emails, and handle delivery, confirmation, and unsubscribe records. Brevo processes the information needed to provide these services on our behalf. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and retention

You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in a marketing email or by contacting kendra@reacnspire.com. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop necessary communications about services you have requested, transactions, or support.

We keep subscription information while needed to manage your subscription. After you unsubscribe or request deletion, we may retain limited consent and unsubscribe records to honor your choice, prevent accidental re-enrollment, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations. These records are not used to continue sending marketing emails. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your information.

Email performance measurement

We use Brevo’s anonymous tracking for marketing and transactional emails. Opens and link clicks are recorded to measure overall email performance, but are not linked to individual contacts in our engagement reports. Anonymous tracking does not mean that no tracking takes place: emails may still use tracking images and tracked links.

Our campaign reports are configured to exclude Apple Mail Privacy Protection-generated opens and detected bot activity. These filters help reduce automated activity in our statistics, but measurements remain estimates. We maintain subscription confirmation, delivery, and unsubscribe records separately as needed to manage your subscription and honor your choices. You can stop future marketing emails using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

Policy changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
