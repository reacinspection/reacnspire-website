Kendra REAC Consultant, Inc. ("Kendra REAC," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy applies to reacnspire.com and information submitted through our consultation, training, registration, and contact forms. Separate products or applications may provide additional privacy notices.

The site may use browser storage to remember language or promotional-message preferences. Analytics providers may use cookies or similar technologies. You can restrict these through your browser, although some features may not work as intended.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, contractual and accounting records, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, or ask us to stop non-essential marketing messages. We may retain records when required for legitimate business, contractual, or legal purposes.
