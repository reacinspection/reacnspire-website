A failed NSPIRE inspection doesn't just cost points — it triggers more frequent inspections, enforcement risk, and pressure on your HUD funding. NSPIRE consulting closes that gap by finding and fixing deficiencies before the inspection, not after.

What Is NSPIRE Inspection Consulting?

NSPIRE inspection consulting is professional guidance that prepares your property to pass its HUD NSPIRE inspection with confidence. Rather than hoping for a good score, you get an expert who inspects the way HUD inspects, documents every deficiency, and builds a realistic plan to correct it in time. Because NSPIRE now weighs health and safety inside the units more heavily than ever, the difference between a passing and a failing score is often the issues an untrained eye walks right past.

At Kendra REAC Consultant, that eye belongs to Sandra Romero-Negron — a HUD REAC certified inspector since 2009 with over 5,600 FEMA disaster-response inspections behind her. Consulting is grounded in how inspections actually run in the field, not theory.

Our NSPIRE Property Assessment Process

Every engagement is shaped to your portfolio, your score history, and your timeline — but the framework is consistent and proven:

  1. Pre-NSPIRE Inspection. We walk your property exactly as a federal inspector would — unit interiors, common areas, and the building exterior — scoring against current NSPIRE standards.
  2. Identify and Prioritize Deficiencies. Every finding is documented with photos and ranked by severity, so your team fixes the score-killers and life-threatening items first.
  3. Corrective Action Planning. You receive a clear, sequenced action plan your maintenance staff can execute — what to fix, in what order, and why it matters to your score.
  4. NSPIRE Staff Training. We teach your team to recognize and prevent the deficiencies we found, in English or Spanish, so readiness sticks between inspections.
  5. Mock NSPIRE Inspection. A full dress rehearsal confirms the corrections hold up and your team knows how to conduct itself on inspection day.
  6. Final Readiness Review. A last pass before the federal inspection to confirm nothing was missed and your documentation is in order.
Why timing matters: the best window to start is 30–60 days before your scheduled NSPIRE inspection — enough time to correct every documented deficiency without a scramble. If your inspection is sooner, urgent timelines can still be accommodated.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NSPIRE inspection consulting?

It's professional guidance that prepares a property to pass its HUD NSPIRE inspection — a pre-inspection walkthrough, deficiency identification and prioritization, a corrective action plan, staff training, and a mock inspection before the federal inspector arrives.

How far before my NSPIRE inspection should I hire a consultant?

Ideally 30–60 days out, so your team has time to correct every documented deficiency. Sooner inspections can still be accommodated on an urgent basis.

Do you work with housing authorities and HUD-assisted owners?

Yes — property managers, public housing authorities, HUD-assisted owners, and affordable housing organizations across the United States and Puerto Rico.

What's the difference between REAC and NSPIRE?

REAC is the HUD office that runs inspections; NSPIRE is the inspection standard that replaced UPCS in 2023, focused on health and safety inside the units. See more on our homepage FAQ.
